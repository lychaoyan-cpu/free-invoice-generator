Invoicing, minus
the paperwork headache.

The Free Invoice Generator exists for a simple reason: getting paid for your work shouldn't require a subscription, an account, or a spreadsheet held together with hope. If you're a freelancer, a contractor, or a small business sending a handful of invoices a month, you need one thing — a clean, professional invoice, right now.

What it does

  • Builds a polished invoice from your details in under a minute, with a live preview as you type.
  • Handles the math for you: line items, percentage or flat discounts, and tax / VAT.
  • Supports ten major currencies with correct formatting, plus your own logo.
  • Turns the result into a print-ready PDF through your browser — no uploads, no waiting.
  • Auto-saves your draft in your browser, so a closed tab never loses your work.

Why it's free

The tool is supported by advertising and, in time, by optional referrals to accounting software for people who outgrow a simple generator. The generator itself stays free and unlimited — that's the deal, and it doesn't expire.

Private by design

Most invoice tools ask you to hand your client list and revenue figures to a server. This one doesn't: everything runs in your browser, and your data never leaves your device. The trade-off is honest — there's no cloud backup and no syncing between devices, just a tool that does its job and keeps nothing. Our Privacy Policy spells it out.
